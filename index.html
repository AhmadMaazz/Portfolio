05 · Publications

Research & publications

01

VGG Models in Image Captioning: Which Architecture Delivers Better Descriptions?

Maaz, A. · Abbas, S. · Ali, R.H. · Ahmed, I. · Khan, T.A. · First & Corresponding Author · ICOSST'24, Lahore, Pakistan · 2025

Compared VGG16 and VGG19 backbones in a custom encoder-decoder architecture for image captioning on the Flickr8k dataset, training each for 30 and 50 epochs to isolate how training duration affects caption quality. BLEU-1 through BLEU-4 scores came out comparable across both architectures, with VGG16 offering slightly faster training and inference at no real cost to caption quality.

0.541 BLEU-1 (VGG16) 0.533 BLEU-1 (VGG19)
Image Captioning VGG16 / VGG19 Encoder-Decoder
Read on IEEE Xplore →
02

Influencing Factors in Facial Recognition and Verification: A Siamese Neural Network Hyperparameter Study

Maaz, A. · Arshad, U. · Ahmad, T. · Abbas, S. · First Author · ICECT'24, Islamabad, Pakistan · 2024

Studied how optimizer choice, loss function, and dataset diversity affect Siamese network accuracy on a facial verification task combining the LFW dataset with a custom-collected negative set. The Adam optimizer paired with Binary Cross-Entropy loss delivered the strongest results, while switching to SGD, Triplet Loss, Contrastive Loss, or adding dropout layers gave no meaningful improvement.

99.8% Recall (Adam) 99.7% Precision (Adam)
Siamese Networks Hyperparameter Tuning Facial Verification
Read on IEEE Xplore →
03

The YOLOv8 Edge: Harnessing Custom Datasets for Superior Real-Time Detection

Ahmed, T. · Maaz, A. · Mahmood, D. · ul Abideen, Z. · Arshad, U. · Ali, R.H. · ICET'23, Peshawar, Pakistan · 2023

Trained a custom YOLOv8 detector on a purpose-built dataset of four everyday object classes (books, keyboards, glasses, and mice), tuning batch size, learning rate, and input resolution. The model outperformed earlier YOLO versions on accuracy, demonstrating the value of domain-specific training data for robotics, surveillance, and autonomous-vehicle applications.

0.864 mAP50 0.758 mAP50-95
