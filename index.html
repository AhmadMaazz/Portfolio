01 · About

A quick introduction

I'm an AI engineer and researcher currently pursuing an MSc in Human-Computer Interaction at the University of Siegen, Germany. My background spans generative AI research, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems, computer vision, and hands-on technical support for AI-powered hardware in production. I've built intelligent tutoring agents, multi-agent self-correcting pipelines, and RAG-based legal assistants — and co-authored three peer-reviewed papers along the way.

I care about systems that are technically rigorous and genuinely usable — which is what drew me toward HCI after a bachelor's in Artificial Intelligence. Outside of research and engineering, I've led student tech communities and mentored teams through hackathons and workshops.
